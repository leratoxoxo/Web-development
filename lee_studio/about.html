About Lee's Studio

Precision, craftsmanship and timeless design.

A modern destination for watch lovers

Lee's Studio is a premium timepiece retail concept based at Mall of Africa in Johannesburg, South Africa.

We specialise in luxury watches from internationally recognised brands, aimed at watch collectors and luxury shoppers who value quality, craftsmanship, precision and timeless design.

Our mission is to provide access to carefully selected timepieces while delivering a sophisticated, trustworthy and personalised shopping experience.
